<p>MTPV shows one PDF on several Android tablets side by side, like extra monitors on a PC: the tablet on the left shows page 1, the next one page 2, and so on. Turning the page moves every screen on by one page. It was made for sheet music on a music stand, but works for any PDF.</p>

<p><b>Only one tablet?</b> MTPV works on its own as a sheet music reader: a folder of PDFs becomes a song set, pages turn by tap or pedal, and your notes stay with each song. Add more tablets whenever you like.</p>

<p>One app takes both roles. The <b>controller</b> opens the PDF and turns the pages; each <b>secondary</b> shows its page. The roles are remembered, so at the next rehearsal you switch the tablets on, start the app, and carry on where you stopped.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>One page per tablet, in any order you arrange them; screens past the last page stay blank</li>
<li>Turn pages by tapping the left or right edge of any screen, or with a Bluetooth page-turn pedal (Page Up/Down, arrow keys)</li>
<li>Song sets: put one PDF per song in a folder of your library and the songs follow each other like one long PDF. Reorder them, or untick songs to skip them; the set keeps its order</li>
<li>Handwritten notes with finger or stylus on every screen: pens in three colours, a marker and an eraser. Notes follow the page to whichever screen shows it and never change the PDF itself</li>
<li>Dark pages for dim stages, with staves and stems as strong as on paper</li>
<li>The PDF is sent to the other tablets automatically and kept there</li>
<li>Open PDFs from any file manager</li>
<li>Export and import of all notes and sets, to move to a new tablet</li>
<li>Pairing: a secondary obeys only controllers that were allowed on its own screen, so it works where other people use MTPV too</li>
<li>Resumes the last PDF, page and screen arrangement after a restart</li>
</ul>

<p><b>No Wi-Fi at the venue?</b> The controller then starts its own direct network (Wi-Fi Direct), and tablets paired at home join it by themselves. No router and no internet connection are needed, and nothing leaves your tablets. Up to Android 12, Android allows this only with the location permission; MTPV asks for it with an explanation and never reads your location.</p>

<p><b>Requirements</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Android 10 or newer</li>
<li>With several tablets: Wi-Fi switched on; a shared Wi-Fi network is used when there is one</li>
<li>MTPV works while it is on screen: nothing runs in the background, so it uses no battery after the gig</li>
</ul>

<p>The connection between the tablets is plain HTTP inside your local network; pairing tokens are not encrypted in transit.</p>
